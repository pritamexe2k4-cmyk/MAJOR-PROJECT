1. The proposed framework should be validated using real paired multimodal clinical datasets instead of relying predominantly on CycleGAN-generated synthetic CT images to establish stronger clinical reliability.

3. The study currently emphasizes classification accuracy but lacks detailed clinical interpretability analysis such as lesion localization validation, Grad-CAM visualization, or radiologist-assisted evaluation.
